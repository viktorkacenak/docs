Use **What's New** to catch up on recent Fayne improvements and newly available features.

## Open What's New

1. Open the **Help** menu in the owner sidebar.
2. Select **What's New**.

New entries appear first. An unread badge lets you know when updates are waiting. Opening What's New marks the current entries as read.

## What's included

Each update explains a product change and where to find it. Use it when a button has moved, a workflow looks different, or you want to see what was added since your last visit.

<Note>
  What's New is for academy owners and team members. The [Feed](/engagement/newsfeed) is the separate space where you publish updates for students.
</Note>
