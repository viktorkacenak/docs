Use **Settings** in the owner sidebar to manage the academy you are currently viewing and your Fayne account.

## Academy settings

| Section | What you can manage |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Academy name, publishing, and Open academy or Invite only access |
| **Domain** | Your Fayne subdomain and custom domain |
| **Email Sending** | Sender name, reply-to address, and custom sending domain |
| **API Keys** | Keys for approved integrations |
| **MCP server** | Connect supported AI tools to this academy |
| **Payments** | Stripe connection and payment settings |
| **Team** | People who help manage this academy |

Academy settings apply only to the academy selected in the switcher.

## Account settings

| Section | What you can manage |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your name and account email |
| **Billing** | Fayne plan, billing period, invoices, and payment method |
| **Referrals** | Your referral link and rewards |
| **Notifications** | Product updates, weekly digest, and re-engagement nudges |

Changing your account email requires confirmation at the new address.

<Note>
  Security messages, student invitations, sign-in codes, and purchase receipts are sent when needed even if optional notification preferences are off.
</Note>

## Delete an academy

The **Danger Zone** in Settings lets the academy owner permanently delete the selected academy. Fayne asks you to type the academy name before confirming.

<Warning>
  Deleting an academy removes its courses, students, progress, and settings. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>
