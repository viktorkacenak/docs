Open **Home** in the owner sidebar for the next setup step or a quick view of how your academy is doing.

## When your academy is new

Home starts with a setup checklist. The recommended order can change to match what you told Fayne during onboarding, but it helps you complete the essentials:

- Personalize a lesson
- Add a course from the Explore Library or build one from scratch
- Customize branding
- Preview the academy
- Invite your first student
- Connect a custom domain

Select the highlighted next step to go to the right screen. Completed steps stay in the checklist as shortcuts.

Home also links to the Explore Library, the AI course drafter, and this help center.

## Your academy plan

If you set up your academy with Fayne's guided setup, Home also shows the plan you approved there. See [Quickstart](/quickstart).

**Your first course** tracks the course Fayne is writing for you, with a status line that updates on its own: "Planning the lessons…", then "Writing lesson 3 of 8", then "Ready · 8 lessons written". Click **Open course** to look at it at any time; lessons appear as they finish. If the outline is waiting for you, the button reads **Review outline**.

**The rest of your academy** lists the other courses from your plan that you haven't built yet ("Planned during setup. Generate any of them when you're ready."). Each card shows the course's promise and an estimate such as "~6 lessons · ~60 credits".

- On a paid plan, click **Generate** to start that course. Fayne writes the brief from your plan and opens the course generation screen while it builds the outline; review it, then click **Draft N lessons**. Drafting draws from your AI credits as usual (about 10 credits per lesson). See [AI course generation](/content/ai-course-generation).
- During the free trial, the cards show **Unlock** and the section offers **Subscribe**: building the rest of your plan needs a paid plan.

A course leaves this list once you build it, and then lives on your **Courses** page like any other.

## After you launch

Once the required setup steps are complete, the dashboard becomes a day-to-day overview.

Use [Activity](/engagement/activity) for the full event timeline and [Insights](/analytics/overview) for detailed filters and charts.

## Invite from Home

Select **Invite students** in the Home header to open the same invitation window used on **People**. You can enter several email addresses and optionally grant courses, offers, or Lists.
