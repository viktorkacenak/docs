Open **Home** in the owner sidebar for the next setup step or a quick view of how your academy is doing.

## When your academy is new

Home starts with a setup checklist. The recommended order can change to match what you told Fayne during onboarding, but it helps you complete the essentials:

- Personalize a lesson
- Add a course from the Explore Library or build one from scratch
- Customize branding
- Preview the academy
- Invite your first student
- Connect a custom domain

Select the highlighted next step to go to the right screen. Completed steps stay in the checklist as shortcuts.

Home also links to the Explore Library, the AI course drafter, and this help center.

## After you launch

Once the required setup steps are complete, the dashboard becomes a day-to-day overview.

Use [Activity](/engagement/activity) for the full event timeline and [Insights](/analytics/overview) for detailed filters and charts.

## Invite from Home

Select **Invite students** in the Home header to open the same invitation window used on **People**. You can enter several email addresses and optionally grant courses, offers, or Lists.
