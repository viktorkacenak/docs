A knowledge check is a lesson made of quiz blocks that is **graded**. Members submit their answers, Fayne scores them on the server, and the result decides whether they pass. A check can be required, so later lessons stay locked until it is passed, or optional, so it simply records a score.

Checks use the same quiz blocks you already know from lessons: multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, ordering, and matching. The difference is what happens on submit. In a normal lesson each block confirms itself instantly. In a check, the answer keys are stripped from what the member receives, and nothing is revealed until they submit the whole check.

## Add a check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the course editor">
    Go to **Courses** and open the course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a knowledge check">
    Open the chevron menu next to **Add lesson** in the lesson sidebar and choose **Add knowledge check**. If the course has [modules](/content/modules), each module header menu offers the same option, so you can drop a check at the end of a chapter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the questions">
    A check opens in the normal block editor. Type `/` and insert blocks from the **Knowledge checks** group: **Multiple Choice**, **True or False**, **Fill in the Blanks**, **Put in Order**, or **Match the Pairs**. Anything else you add (headings, a short intro) is shown but not graded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the scoring">
    Open **Check settings** from the check's row in the sidebar.

    - **Pass mark**: the share of questions that must be correct to pass. Default is **70%**.
    - **Required to advance**: when on, later lessons stay locked until this check is passed. On by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the check like any lesson. Checks show a distinct icon in the lesson list so you can tell them apart from lessons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A check needs at least one gradeable quiz block. A check with only text has nothing to score, so members cannot pass it.
</Tip>

## What members experience

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Submit once, see the result" icon="send">
    Members answer every question, then press **Submit answers**. They see their score against the pass mark and either **Check passed** or **Not quite there yet**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Retake freely" icon="rotate-cw">
    A failed check can be retaken. The best score is what counts, so a later failed attempt never undoes an earlier pass.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Locked lessons" icon="lock">
    When a check is required, the lessons after it show as locked in the sidebar and the course outline with a "Pass ... to continue" note. Passing unlocks them immediately.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Completion" icon="flag">
    Passing a required check marks it complete. An optional check counts as complete on submit, whatever the score.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Checks combine with [drip scheduling](/content/drip-scheduling). If a lesson is behind both a required check and a drip timer, the check wins while it is unpassed; once passed, the drip timer applies as usual. Work a member has already completed never re-locks when you change settings later.

<Note>
  Answers are graded on the server against the stored check, and the answer keys never reach the browser before submit. Members cannot read the correct answers out of the page.
</Note>

## Read the results

Open a course's insights (the chart icon next to the settings gear in the course editor, or **Insights** in the owner sidebar and then the course) and switch to the **Knowledge checks** tab. A **Graded checks** section at the top lists every check with:

- its pass mark and whether it is required to advance
- **pass rate**: the share of members who have ever passed it
- **average best score** across members
- an expandable list of members with their latest score, best score, and number of tries, with members who have not yet passed sorted first

See [Analytics](/analytics/overview) for the rest of the course insights.

## Select all that apply

A **Multiple Choice** question can ask for one answer or several. In the block's settings, set **Answers** to **Single** (the default) or **Multiple**.

- **Single**: members pick one option. If you mark more than one option correct, any one of them counts.
- **Multiple**: members tick every option that applies and see "Select all that apply" under the question. The answer counts as right only when they pick every correct option and nothing else. They are never told how many options are correct.

In a normal lesson, a member who answers a **Multiple** question sees which picks were wrong and which correct options they **Missed**. Inside a check, nothing is revealed until they submit. Switching between **Single** and **Multiple** keeps the answers you marked.

In course insights, each option a member ticked counts once, so you can see which wrong options people tend to add.

## Checks from AI generation

When you [generate a course with AI](/content/ai-course-generation), the brief's **Knowledge checks** setting decides how many checks the outline gets: **None**, **Some** (a short check after each module), or **Many** (a check every two or three lessons). A check is never first or doubled up. It is written from the finished lessons it covers, so its questions match what was actually taught: three to five questions in a micro course, four to eight otherwise. Checks show a **Check** label on the outline, and you can remove or move them like any lesson.
