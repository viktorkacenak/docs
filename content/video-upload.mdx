Add video from YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom, or upload a video file directly to a lesson.

## Add a video

1. Open **Courses** and select a course.
2. Open the lesson you want to edit.
3. Type `/` and choose **Video**.
4. Choose one option:
   - Paste a YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom link. Loom's **Copy link** URL works as is.
   - Upload an MP4, WebM, or Ogg video file.
5. Wait for the upload to finish, then save the lesson.

Uploaded videos can be up to 1 GB. Keep the editor open while the progress indicator is visible. If an upload is interrupted, use the retry option; only the failed parts are re-sent.

## Add captions to an uploaded video

Select an uploaded video block to open its settings and add a **captions** file. Captions must be in WebVTT format (a `.vtt` file). Students get a captions control in the player. Raw `.srt` files are not supported by browsers, so convert them to `.vtt` first.

## Replace or remove a video

Select the video block to open its settings. Replace the link or file, or remove the block from the lesson. Save the lesson after the change.

## Make it available to students

A saved lesson can still be a draft. Publish the lesson when it is ready, and confirm that its course is also published.

Lessons that contain a video show a video icon in the course outline and the lesson sidebar, so students can spot them.

## Troubleshooting

- **The file is rejected:** Use MP4, WebM, or Ogg and keep it at or below 1 GB.
- **The upload stops:** Check your connection, keep the tab open, and retry.
- **Students cannot play it:** Confirm the lesson and course are published, then check the student's course access.
- **A pasted link does not load:** Use the public YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom video URL rather than an editing or studio URL.
- **Captions do not show:** Confirm the file is `.vtt`, not `.srt`.
