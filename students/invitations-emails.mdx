Invite students from **People** and help them sign in with the same email address you invited.

## Invite students

1. Open **People**.
2. Select **Add student**.
3. Enter one or more email addresses.
4. Optionally choose courses or offers to grant.
5. Send the invitation.

An invitation is valid for 30 days. A student with an existing Fayne account joins with that account. A new student creates an account from the invitation.

<Warning>
  The email address used to accept an invitation must match the invited address.
</Warning>

## How students sign in

Fayne uses a six-digit sign-in code instead of a password.

1. The student enters their email address on the academy sign-in page.
2. Fayne emails a six-digit code.
3. The student enters the code to continue.

If a code does not arrive, ask the student to check spam, wait briefly, and resend it. For an Invite only academy, they must use the invited email address.

## Emails students may receive

Fayne sends messages when they are relevant, including:

- Academy invitation and welcome
- Signup confirmation and sign-in code
- Course assigned or access revoked
- Academy access removed
- Course or offer purchased
- New course published
- Certificate issued

## Who the email comes from

Your academy name is used as the sender name. Until you verify a custom sending domain, the email address uses Fayne's shared sending domain. In **Settings \> Email Sending**, you can set a reply-to address and connect your own sending domain on a paid plan.
