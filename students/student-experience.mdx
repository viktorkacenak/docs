Everything your members see is your academy: your name, your logo, your colors, your subdomain, and even the word for them (members by default, or clients, students, participants, or learners, as you set under [Customize](/academy/branding)). This page walks through what that experience feels like from their side so you know exactly what you are handing them. It covers the dashboard they land on, the lesson viewer they learn in, the study tools they collect along the way, and the finish line when they complete a course.

For who can see what (open signup, invitations, paid courses), see [Access and Progress](/students/enrollments). For the optional study helper, see the [Learning Assistant](/learning-assistant/overview).

## Light and dark appearance

You choose the academy-wide appearance under **Customize → Colors**. Set it to **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**. System follows each member's device preference. Members do not change this setting inside the academy, so upload both a normal logo and a dark-mode logo if your brand needs different artwork on dark backgrounds.

## My Learning dashboard

My Learning is the member's home base. It groups their enrolled courses into **In Progress** and **Completed**, each shown as a course card with a progress count. Any certificates they have earned appear in a **Certificates** section lower on the same page.

At the top, when a member has started but not finished a course, a **Continue learning** banner highlights the course they are partway through, names the next lesson ("Up next: ..."), shows a progress ring, and gives them a single **Continue** button straight to that lesson. It points at the first lesson they have not yet completed and can open, so a returning member is one click from where they left off.

<Note>
  If a member has not started anything yet, My Learning shows a friendly empty state with a **Browse Courses** button instead of cards.
</Note>

## The course catalog

The catalog shows your courses grouped by your collections, under the hero you configured (a Continue banner, a featured course, or your own banner). Any custom links you added appear in the sidebar. When you sell through [offers](/payments/offers), it grows two extra shelves: one with cards for offers the member could buy (bundles and the Academy Pass link to their product pages), and **Your access**, listing the offers they already hold. Free courses carry a **Free** chip once the academy sells anything. On a locked course's page, members see the ways in: a buy button, a comparison of the offers that cover it, your custom access link, or a note that access is managed by the academy when there is nothing to buy.

### How lessons get marked complete

Members do not have to hunt for a button. A lesson is marked complete automatically in two ways:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scrolling to the end">
    When the member reads to the bottom of a lesson, it is marked complete and the sidebar checkmark animates in. There is a small guard so this only fires after they have actually scrolled through the lesson, not the instant the page loads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Moving to the next lesson">
    Clicking **Next Lesson** (or **Complete Course** on the last one) also marks the current lesson complete, so members who skim still get credit for what they moved past.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Knowledge checks and surveys are the exception: a check completes when the member submits it, and a required check only once they pass. A [survey](/content/surveys) completes when it is submitted, and its answers can't be changed afterwards.

The course-level progress bar in the sidebar updates as lessons are completed, and that same progress is what drives the Continue banner and the course cards on My Learning.

## In-lesson study tools

While reading, members get a small toolbar for capturing what matters. Everything they save is private to them, with one exception you control: reflections you set to share with the academy (see below).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Save (bookmark)" icon="bookmark">
    One tap saves the current lesson so they can return to it later.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Notes" icon="sticky-note">
    A free-form note per lesson. It autosaves as they type, so there is no save button to remember.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Highlights" icon="highlighter">
    Members highlight passages as they read. Highlights are course-wide and grouped by lesson in the panel, so they can jump back to any highlighted spot across the whole course.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reflections" icon="pencil">
    When a lesson includes an authored reflection prompt, the member's written answer is captured with it. A private reflection tells them "Your answers are only visible to you". If you turn on **Share answers with the academy** for a reflection, that line is not shown and you can read what they write there.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### The Saved page

All of the above is gathered in one place: a page labeled **Saved** in the member navigation. It reads as a single workbook stream of their bookmarks, notes, highlights, and reflection answers, each linking back to the lesson it came from. Items tied to a course the member no longer has access to are quietly dropped, so the workbook stays clean.

<Tip>
  Saved is a strong retention surface. The more a member annotates, the more reason they have to come back to your academy rather than re-finding the content elsewhere.
</Tip>

## Achievements and collectible cards

The **Achievements** page turns finishing a course into something tangible. Every course in your academy has its own collectible card:

- Courses the member has **completed** show their earned card, personalized with the member's name, the course title, lesson count, total minutes, and completion date, all on your academy branding.
- Courses they have **not** finished show a **locked** placeholder card, so members can see the full set there is to collect.

The page header tracks the count, for example "You've collected 3 of 12 course cards." Earned certificates also surface here in a Certificates section.

## Certificates

When a course is set up to issue a certificate, completing it generates one for the member. Certificates appear on My Learning, on the Achievements page, and on the member's profile, each linking to a public verification page. Members can download the certificate as a PDF.

For how you turn certificates on and what they contain, see [Certificates](/students/certificates).

Finishing a course drops the member onto a celebratory completion screen: "Well done, [name]!", the course title, and their collectible card. From here they can:

## Member account and Purchases

Members get a profile page (their name, avatar, a preview of their achievements, and certificates) and an account area for your academy.

- **Purchases** lists everything the member has bought, whether a single course, a bundle, or the Academy Pass, each with its status, the date and amount, an invoice number when present, an **Invoice PDF** button, and a **View receipt** link. If a member holds a recurring offer, its subscription appears here too, with the price and renewal date. They can cancel it themselves (access runs to the end of the paid period) and resume it before it ends, without contacting you. Members who have not bought anything see an empty state.
- **Account** lets a member leave your academy. Leaving is confirmation-gated (they type their email) and only removes them from this academy; their underlying account and any other academies they belong to are untouched, and their progress is kept in case you invite them back.

<Info>
  Purchases and receipts only come into play once you sell courses, which requires a connected Stripe account on a paid plan. See [Stripe Connect](/payments/stripe-connect).
</Info>

## Guest browsing on open academies

On an open (non-invite) academy, a visitor without an account is not slammed into a login wall. They can browse the course catalog and individual course pages, and they can look at the **My Learning**, **Achievements**, and **Saved** sections too. Instead of personal data, those sections render a clean sign-in nudge ("Your courses live here", "Start your collection", "Build your personal workbook") with **Sign up free** and **Sign in** buttons.

This means a shared academy link shows off a real, branded product, what there is to learn and what there is to earn, before anyone commits to an account.

<Warning>
  Guest browsing applies to open academies. Invite-only academies show a private-academy screen to anyone who is not a signed-in, invited member, and the profile and account pages always require sign-in. Enrolling and opening actual lesson content always require an account.
</Warning>
